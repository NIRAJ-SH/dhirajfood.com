ABOUT US

Trusted Since 2000

Dhiraj Food is one of Nepal's trusted importers and distributors of premium dry fruits, spices, pulses, and food products. Founded in 2000 by Shree Bhagwan Sah, the company has grown from a local business in Birgunj, Nepal into a recognized brand known for quality, trust, and customer satisfaction. We offer 35+ premium products, including Cashew Nuts, Almonds, Raisins (Kismis), Jeera, Dhaniya, Mishri, Loung, Sukmel and a wide range of dry fruits and spices. Every product is carefully sourced, hygienically packed, and quality-checked to ensure freshness, purity, and exceptional taste.

With a strong distribution network, Dhiraj Food proudly supplies households, retailers, wholesalers, and businesses across Nepal. Our commitment to premium quality, hygienic packaging, and reliable service has made us a trusted name in the Nepali market for over three decades
